The Intersection of
Silence and Sound.

My creative journey is built on two pillars: the movement of the body and the weight of the written word. As a dancer, I explore the narratives that live in the muscles and the spaces between beats. As a spoken word poet, I give breath to the raw, unspoken truths of the human experience. I believe that dance and poetry are two sides of the same coin—both are attempts to make sense of what we feel but cannot always name.

Whether I am on stage performing a poem or moving through a choreography, my goal remains the same: to create a space for vulnerability, expression, and connection. I don't just perform; I tell stories that resonate in the heart and settle in the soul.
